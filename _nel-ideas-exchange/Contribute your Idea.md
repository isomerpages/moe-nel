<h2>Join the NEL Community today!</h2>
<p>Why join the NEL Community?</p>
<p>The NEL Community is a group of pre-school educators who use the NEL resources
and share good practices.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="iframe-wrapper">
<iframe height="315" width="560" allowfullscreen="true" frameborder="0" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/2dSuCaQRe5A?si=cm1Gc7AGjXLnD3it"></iframe>
</div>
<p></p>
<p>As part of the NEL Community, you will be able to&nbsp;</p>
<p>1. Share your centre’s good practices;&nbsp;</p>
<p>2. Provide a platform for the professional development of your teachers
through engagement with MOE officers;&nbsp;</p>
<p>3. Be recognised and receive 2 CPD hours upon successful contribution
to the NEL Portal; and&nbsp;</p>
<p>4. Network with other like-minded professionals.</p>
<h3><strong>How can I be part of the NEL Community?&nbsp;</strong></h3>
<p>Contribute your ideas, so that we can learn and share with one another!&nbsp;</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Do you have activity ideas with a distinctive Singapore flavour?&nbsp;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Would you be able to contribute a write-up, with photographs to show the
main activity stages?&nbsp;</p>
</li>
</ul>
